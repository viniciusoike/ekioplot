# Gallery

Charts built with `ekioplot` for EKIO’s data visualization work. Click a
chart to open it at full size.

## EKIO-viz

[EKIO-viz](https://github.com/viniciusoike/ekio-viz) collects standalone
charts on the Brazilian economy, cities, and society. Each chart has its
R script in the repository.
